# CEOWEB Interactive Demo Walkthrough (3-Minute Script)

Welcome to the evaluator walkthrough of **CEOWEB**! Follow these quick steps to explore the product end to end.

---

## 🏁 Step 1: Launch Local App
```bash
npm run dev
```
Open **[http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000)** (or the port displayed in terminal, e.g. 3001).

Notice the bottom-right badge:
- `BACKEND: MOCK STORE`: Active zero-config evaluation mode.

---

## 🥋 Step 2: Feed, Mood Filtering, and Ephemeral Stories
1. **Stories Bar**: Tap any story bubble (e.g. Maya or Alex) at the top of the feed. Notice the Snapchat-style auto-advancing progress segments and view count.
2. **Post Creator**: Type a post and select a mood tag (`🎨 Creative` or `📚 Study & Code`). Click **Post to Wall** — observe your XP bar jump (+25 XP).
3. **Mood Feed**: Click `📚 Study & Code` or `🍃 Chill` to dynamically filter feed posts.
4. **Dojo Focus Mode**: In the left sidebar, click **Focus Mode (Healthy Use)**. Select a 15/30/60 minute timer to shield distractions.

---

## 💬 Step 3: Chat, Voice Notes, and LiveKit Calls
1. Navigate to **Messages** in the sidebar.
2. Select **Maya Lin**.
3. **Voice Note**: Tap the cyan microphone icon, record for a few seconds, and tap send. Hear the recorded waveform/playback.
4. **Round Video Circle**: Tap the pink video icon to preview and send a circular video note.
5. **LiveKit Calling**: Tap the Phone or Video icon in the chat header to launch the active call HUD with microphone/camera toggles and duration timer.

---

## 📡 Step 4: Live Broadcasts & Friends Map
1. **Live Streams**: Navigate to `/live` in the sidebar. View the low-latency broadcast stage, stream chat, and click **Send Spark** to award free community sparks.
2. **Friends Radar (Snap Map style)**: Navigate to `/map`. View friends across Tokyo and Berkeley. Toggle **Ghost Mode** to inspect instant location shielding.

---

## 📈 Step 5: Executive Settings & Analytics
1. Navigate to **Settings & Privacy** (`/settings`).
2. Adjust privacy sliders (who can call me, who can message me).
3. Click **Export JSON** to verify data portability compliance.
4. Visit your profile to view your **Creator Analytics** charts and Belt/Career Rank progression.
